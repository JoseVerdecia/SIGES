This guide summarizes the system's main functional modules.

## Indicators

Management of institutional indicators, including their descriptive information, goals, and results required for monitoring and evaluation.

## Processes

Management of university processes and consolidation of information related to their indicators.

## Areas

Management of responsible areas, including faculties and municipalities.

## Strategic Objectives

Organization and monitoring of institutional strategic objectives and their relationship with indicators and processes.

## Goals and Results

Recording of goals and actual results to facilitate performance tracking.

## Evaluation

Application of business rules to automatically classify indicator performance according to its level of compliance.

## Users and Roles

User management with different responsibilities within the application.

Roles documented in the system:

- Administrator.
- Process Manager.
- Area Manager.
- Standard User.

## Summaries

Consolidation of information to obtain an overall view of the status of processes and strategic objectives.

## API

The project documents a REST service layer to support the system's operations.
