## Project Title
**SIGES — Indicator and Goal Management System**

## Description for the Projects Section

I developed SIGES as my Computer Engineering thesis project at the University of Holguín, Faculty of Computer Science and Mathematics, Oscar Lucero Moya campus.

SIGES is a web application designed to digitize and centralize the management of institutional indicators and goals. The system allows users to manage indicators, processes, areas, and strategic objectives; record actual results; define goals; apply automated evaluations; and consolidate information to support decision-making.

The project allowed me to work with C#, .NET, Blazor Server, ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework Core, SQL Server, and FluentUI Blazor, as well as business rule design, role management, and REST operations.
